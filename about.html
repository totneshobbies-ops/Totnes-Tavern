The Saga of the Tavern

Every great odyssey begins with a single spark. For the Tavern, that spark was struck years ago in the quiet corners of a private collection. What began as a humble endeavor—the trading of duplicate Pokémon scrolls—soon ignited into a grand vision for a sanctuary.

Like a small flame growing into a roaring hearth, the Tavern expanded its horizons. We no longer just trade in cards; we provide the steel and sorcery for every traveler's journey. From the battlefields of Wargaming to the realms of Dungeons & Dragons, we supply the miniatures, hobby paints, and TCGs required to forge your destiny.

The Great Halls of Strategy

A traveler cannot wander forever; every fellowship needs a place to rest their boots and sharpen their blades. Within the Tavern walls, we have raised Great Halls of Strategy—dedicated tables where generals lead armies to glory and parties map out their next dungeon crawl.

Should you seek a sanctuary for your DnD campaign or a fortified position for Wargaming, our tables are available for hire. Secure your place in history by claiming a seat at our hearth.

"Draw your chair, unroll your maps, and let the dice decide your fate."

Meet the Tavern Master

Behind the counter sits Tommy, the keeper of this realm. A veteran of a thousand tabletop wars and a seeker of the rarest holos, Tommy’s passion was forged in the fires of his youth.

From his earliest days, he was captivated by the strategic depth of Warhammer, the storytelling of DnD, and the thrill of the Pokémon hunt. Today, he translates that lifelong obsession into the Totnes Hobby Tavern—a place where beginners are welcomed as heroes.

"I didn't just want to build a shop; I wanted to build the headquarters for every adventure in Totnes." — Tommy
